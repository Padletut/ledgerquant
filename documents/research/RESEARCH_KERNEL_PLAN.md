# Research data readiness and Research Kernel plan

**Date:** 09 October 2026

**Status:** Bounded EURUSD and GBPUSD server-tick exports exist, and EURUSD CLI rows match cTrader Desktop in sampled windows. EURUSD has 2020 and 2021 source-presence measurements; GBPUSD has January 2020 daily-presence measurements. Five 2020 EURUSD pauses of at least ten minutes remain schedule-unverified, and broker quote/cost semantics remain open. The first EURUSD price-direction diagnostic narrowly passed its July–December 2020 predictive gate, but its frozen full-year 2021 replication failed temporal validation. Both are retrospective price-only results. No production Research Kernel, enforced holdout isolation or certified economic result exists yet.

This plan starts with broker-data evidence. The initial **source measurement** scope is IC Markets EU live account ACCOUNT_REDACTED, EURUSD and GBPUSD, from 2020 onward. It does not force every hypothesis to use both pairs or every tick. Each hypothesis freezes its own instrument universe and required information before independent validation. Other accounts, symbols and asset classes enter only after separate source checks. The target architecture and authority boundaries remain in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md); measured source limits remain in [DATA_AVAILABILITY.md](../data/DATA_AVAILABILITY.md).

The readiness question is whether the information state at the registered decision time is sufficient, causally available and faithfully represented for that specific decision and payoff contract. Historical price coverage, raw news availability, semantic derivation and cost evidence answer different parts of that question.

## 1. Characterize source observations

1. **Record source identity and timing.** Identify broker entity, account environment, server where obtainable, exact symbol, cTrader/CLI version, data mode, quote units and timestamp semantics. Keep broker ticks separate from Dukascopy and other external prices. For news, retain the raw artifact, revision, publication time, first-seen evidence and actual `available_at`. Semantic labels and sentiment are derived observations with their own model/prompt version, computation time and availability; they never inherit the raw article's publication time.
2. **Preserve bounded price extracts and gaps.** Use the [bounded exporter](../data/TICK_EXPORT.md) with warm-up and end witnesses, stable source order, immutable hashes and overlap checks. The 1 January backtest without warm-up missed its first observed tick. Record daily and intraday presence, invalid or crossed quotes, timestamp order, equal-time updates and pauses. Exclude the Desktop file's 2018–2019 four-record-per-hour prefix from tick-level history. A source date, CLI argument or successful backtest is not a coverage certificate.
3. **Measure quote and calendar uncertainty.** The [EURUSD 2020 report](../data/measurements/icm-eu-live-eurusd-desktop-2020-daily-coverage.json), [EURUSD 2021 source-presence report](../data/measurements/icm-eu-live-eurusd-desktop-2021-source-coverage/coverage.json), [GBPUSD January report](../data/measurements/icm-eu-live-gbpusd-2020-01-daily-presence.json) and [five-gap cross-check](../data/measurements/icm-eu-live-eurusd-2020-intraday-gap-crosscheck.json) are source measurements. Retain exact unresolved intervals and distinguish observed quotes from dated broker closures. Current `MarketHours.Sessions` or published hours cannot alone certify historical sessions. EURUSD's many equal bid/ask rows still require an independent broker quote/cost contract before net trading outcomes can be trusted.

This stage produces a versioned source inventory with hashes, coverage intervals, gaps and limitations. It does not declare one global start year or require near-complete tick history before any bounded hypothesis can be examined. A wider backfill remains useful for reuse and breadth testing; its scope follows an explicit acquisition plan, not an unstated universal quality threshold.

## 2. Freeze the hypothesis and data-sufficiency policy

Before independent validation, register the decision, event-selection rule, feature/source, payoff, cost, development and validation windows, instruments, minimum support, success/failure gates and trial budget. Generate the event candidate set from the frozen source and selection rule without consulting future price outcomes. Freeze a `data_sufficiency_policy` with requirements for three different roles:

The first bounded contract is [EURUSD four-hour direction, 2020 v1](hypotheses/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1.json), with a separate [content-hash freeze record](hypotheses/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1.freeze.json). It fixes one deterministic prior-hour momentum question, January–June development, July–December validation, a development-frozen constant-direction baseline, source and quote sampling rules, missing-case handling, minimum support and a paired uncertainty gate. Its claim is **predictive only**: no order is placed and no net-payoff or promotion conclusion is permitted. The freeze record is an offline preregistration artifact, not the future research registry or an evaluation result. The audit and evaluation artifacts below complete this first bounded diagnostic; they do not upgrade its evidence mode.

| Data role | Required declaration | What makes a case ineligible or uncertain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Decision information** | Decision anchor after required inference, actual or assumed inference latency, raw news and revisions, observed versus retrospective semantic mode, model/prompt binding, price lookback, temporal resolution, maximum age and actual or hypothetical visibility rule. | Missing or late raw news, a supposedly historical semantic feature that was not then available, stale predecision prices, or a gap that prevents a required feature. |
| **Outcome measurement** | Settlement rule, horizon, price sampling and tolerance, and whether the payoff depends on the intervening path. | Missing settlement observation, an unobserved stop/limit path, or an outcome assembled from future information. |
| **Economic execution** | Account-specific spread, commission, slippage, fill/rejection and financing assumptions with source and stress ranges. | No credible cost or quote evidence for a claimed net payoff; a price-direction diagnostic may still be reported separately. |

The policy also fixes how to abstain, exclude or mark an event unmeasurable, and the minimum eligible count by period and instrument. Count every proposed event and the reason each case is lost; a filtered survivor set cannot silently become the original universe. A gap away from the required decision, entry, path and settlement windows is still reported, but need not invalidate that case. Never fill a gap with a future quote, treat missing prices as unchanged, or revise tolerance after inspecting validation outcomes.

For example, a four-hour directional question after an ECB statement may need the statement version actually accessible at the decision anchor, a bounded prior-hour EURUSD return and a valid price observation near the frozen settlement time. It need not require every intervening tick if no stop, limit or path-dependent payoff is claimed. A strategy that trades immediately or uses protective exits still needs the quote path and execution-cost evidence required by its contract. This example is an information-state design, not a registered LedgerQuant hypothesis or a claim of economic value.

Historical tick backfill has old **event time** but a later **retrieval/ingest time**. Strict historical replay uses actual `available_at`; a 2026 download cannot enter a 2020 replay. A retrospective simulation may instead apply a frozen, source-specific visibility assumption to backfilled broker prices while preserving their actual later availability. Raw news needs separate first-seen or release evidence for its assumed decision-time eligibility. A pinned model applied now to eligible raw 2020 news is a labelled retrospective inference experiment: store simulated decision time, actual inference time, model version and assumed inference latency separately. The newly computed semantic result was not itself observed in 2020, and the model may have learned about later events during training. Report those limits and seek prospective confirmation; this is neither recorded-output replay nor proof of production-equivalent execution. cTrader sentiment first recorded in October 2026 cannot be a 2020 input. GDELT's archive gaps do not invalidate every selected 2020 event, but its article-level timing uncertainty prevents a general claim that GDELT supplied a complete point-in-time 2020 news feed.

## 3. Materialize the eligible view and build one Kernel slice

Create a bounded dataset view for the frozen hypothesis. Name exact source and chunk hashes, account/feed, symbols, UTC intervals, raw news artifact and revision IDs, semantic derivation versions, canonicalizers and gap report. Enforce actual `available_at` for replay, or the frozen hypothetical visibility rule for a clearly labelled retrospective simulation; never overwrite actual availability. Construct outcomes separately from later prices. Preserve equal-timestamp updates in source order where ticks are required. Missing artifacts or checksums fail closed; derived bars and features retain source references. Evaluate the policy against each proposed case before measuring its outcome and retain both eligible and ineligible case records.

For the first price-only diagnostic, the recorded run used `PYTHONPATH=src python -m ledgerquant.research.cli --repo-root . --freeze documents/research/hypotheses/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1.freeze.json --output-dir documents/research/measurements/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1` to verify the frozen contract and entire source CSV before publishing [hashed input and eligibility files with a manifest](measurements/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1/manifest.json). The publisher refuses to overwrite an existing output directory; a repeat run needs a new destination. The decision-input file holds eligible midquotes with source line and timestamp references and no settlement fields. A separate evaluator-facing eligibility file reports settlement presence and delay, never settlement price, prediction or return. The full 4,734,455,043-byte source matched its registered SHA-256, and the 2020 scan found 17,504,350 rows, matching the earlier daily-presence measurement. All 786 predeclared weekday anchors are retained: development has 386 `MEASURABLE`, four `INPUT_MISSING` and zero `OUTCOME_MISSING`; validation has 391, three and two respectively. Both windows meet this contract's minimum count and maximum missingness gates. The missing cases include New Year's Day, 24–25 and 31 December, and a stale 24 January input; these statuses describe observed quote availability, not certified broker closures. The five previously observed intraday pauses remain schedule-unverified. At audit publication, no outcome values or validation performance had been computed. The local CSV does not enforce holdout access isolation by itself.

One source-backed view with enough eligible cases for its registered question can support the first end-to-end offline research path; complete multi-year tick coverage is required only if that hypothesis needs it. A predictive result without credible execution costs stays a diagnostic, while a net-payoff result requires its frozen cost contract to pass independent stress tests. The vertical slice should contain:

- an immutable hypothesis contract covering decision, feature, payoff, cost, data sufficiency, symbols, development/validation windows, minimum support, success/failure gates and trial budget;
- deterministic, point-in-time bounded dataset access and a candidate calculation over the frozen development window;
- an independent evaluator that alone reads frozen validation outcomes and writes typed measurements, case eligibility, uncertainty, costs, tails, breadth and failure reasons;
- an append-only trial and evidence record, including rejected/null outcomes and exact dataset/code/contract hashes;
- a read-only report that distinguishes development, historical validation and any later prospective evidence.

The initial slice uses a deterministic candidate to verify data access, validation phase separation and evidence lineage before connecting Critic, Research or Discovery agents. Cost accounting and enforced evaluator isolation remain future work for economic hypotheses. Loop A then varies one registered component under a fixed payoff contract. Loop B introduces a new frozen payoff contract before independent validation. Neither an agent nor an optimizer may redefine success after observing holdout results. Shadow, demo and live promotion remain separate later workflows.

### First offline historical validation

The single registered trial used `PYTHONPATH=src python -m ledgerquant.research.evaluation_cli prepare --repo-root . --freeze documents/research/hypotheses/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1.freeze.json --audit-dir documents/research/measurements/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1 --selection-dir documents/research/evaluations/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1/selection` to verify the frozen source and audit, resolve only January–June settlement prices, and publish the [hashed selection record](evaluations/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1/selection/selection.json). The development majority target was `PREDICT_NON_UP` (196 of 386 measurable cases), fixing the baseline. The selection froze all 396 July–December candidate and baseline predictions without validation targets. The separate `evaluate` command verified those hashes before resolving July–December settlement prices and publishing the [typed evidence record](evaluations/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1/evaluation/evidence.json) and [all 396 validation cases](evaluations/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1/evaluation/validation_cases.jsonl). Both publishers refuse to overwrite their output directories.

Of 396 scheduled validation anchors, 391 were measurable, three were `INPUT_MISSING` and two were `OUTCOME_MISSING`; all five missing cases were in December. The candidate was correct on 205 of 391 cases (52.43%) and the baseline on 181 of 391 (46.29%), a paired advantage of 6.14 percentage points. The preregistered UTC-date block bootstrap used 10,000 resamples, seed 20261009 and the nearest-rank fifth percentile; its lower bound was **0.26 percentage points**, just above the frozen zero threshold. The recorded gate is therefore `PREDICTIVE_VALIDATION_PASSED`. The split is uneven: Q3's paired advantage was 11.11 points across 198 cases, while Q4's was 1.04 points across 193 cases (candidate accuracy 48.70%). Mean forward midquote change by predicted class is reported in the evidence record as a price diagnostic, not a return or trading payoff. Monthly case-status counts remain in the [case-audit manifest](measurements/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2020_v1/manifest.json).

This one retrospective pair and half-year does not establish stable transport, instrument breadth, calibrated probability or net economic value. The CSV was obtained in 2026; actual 2020 `available_at` is unknown. Equal bid/ask observations and broker execution costs remain unresolved. The preparation and evaluation commands separate when outcomes are parsed and freeze output hashes, but both run in the same local repository with access to the raw CSV; holdout isolation is a process convention, not an access control. July–December 2020 outcomes are now inspected and must never be reused as untouched validation for a revised rule, threshold, source or hypothesis. A new economic claim needs a child contract with credible quote/execution costs, a new untouched period or prospective evidence, and independent evaluation. Do not promote or optimize this diagnostic against the consumed window.

### Full-year 2021 temporal replication

The next complete calendar year was selected as a new validation window after the 2020 result was inspected, but before any 2021 prediction outcomes were measured. The [source-presence scan](../data/measurements/icm-eu-live-eurusd-desktop-2021-source-coverage/coverage.json) verified the same 4,734,455,043-byte CSV and found 13,412,772 rows on 312 UTC dates in 2021; 1 January was the only weekday without rows. It reported no candidate prediction, return or settlement target. The new [child contract](hypotheses/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2021_replication_v1.json) and [freeze record](hypotheses/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2021_replication_v1.freeze.json) retain the January–June 2020 development window, decision rule, baseline rule, quote sampling, missing-case policy, trial budget and zero lower-bound gate. Only identity, parent lineage and the validation interval changed to 1 January 2021 through 1 January 2022. The freeze records the prevalidation coverage hash and parent evidence already known at registration.

The [2021 case audit](measurements/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2021_replication_v1/manifest.json) retained 783 weekday anchors. Development again had 386 measurable of 390 scheduled; 2021 had 778 measurable, three missing inputs on 1 January and two missing settlement quotes at the 16:00 anchors on 24 and 31 December. Both windows passed the frozen data-sufficiency gates. The [selection record](evaluations/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2021_replication_v1/selection/selection.json) froze the same `PREDICT_NON_UP` baseline and all 783 candidate/baseline predictions before the evaluator resolved 2021 settlement prices. The [typed evaluation evidence](evaluations/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2021_replication_v1/evaluation/evidence.json) retains [all 783 cases](evaluations/eurusd_four_hour_direction_2021_replication_v1/evaluation/validation_cases.jsonl), including failures.

The candidate was correct on **367 of 778** measurable cases (47.17%); the frozen baseline was correct on **384 of 778** (49.36%). The paired difference was **−2.19 percentage points**, and the preregistered daily-block bootstrap fifth-percentile lower bound was **−6.44 points**. The recorded gate is `TEMPORAL_FAILED` with `TEMPORAL_FAILURE`. Q1, Q3 and Q4 had negative paired differences; Q2 was only +0.51 points. The narrow 2020 predictive pass therefore did **not** transport to 2021 under this exact rule and comparator. Both evidence records say one trial within their respective frozen hypotheses; the research family now contains two inspected attempts. The 2021 outcome window is consumed and cannot be relabelled untouched for a revision.

This is evidence against this registered price-direction rule under the retrospective broker-price assumption; it does not rule out other decisions or prove that any agent, news source or sentiment feature lacks value. The 2021 CSV still has no actual historical `available_at`, and 79.88% of its rows have identical bid/ask fields. No executable spread, commission, slippage, fill or net-payoff claim was tested. Before an economic hypothesis, resolve broker quote/cost semantics and freeze a genuinely new decision/payoff and untouched validation plan. Any future Loop A or B search must count both inspected attempts and keep the 2020 and 2021 validation outcomes out of its claimed untouched evidence.
