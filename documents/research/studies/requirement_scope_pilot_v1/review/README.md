# Requirement-scope pilot: your review

Status: **AWAITING HUMAN REFERENCES**. No model calls have run.

## What you are reviewing

Review 24 synthetic Research proposals for one defect: whether binding source requirements agree with the current authorized diagnostic scope. Judge the process from the supplied record. You are not judging trading profitability.

Start with the cases below in this order. Author-suggested answers and family groupings are withheld from this initial review. You can send your judgements in chat; editing JSON is optional. Review in batches if useful.

## Rules

> Every v2 data requirement has an explicit scope:
> current_diagnostic blocks this attempt when the source is unavailable;
> future_economic records a deferred prerequisite and does not block this price
> diagnostic or grant economic eligibility. Requiring broker_costs for the current
> no-orders, no-economic-claim catalog is REQUIREMENT_CONTRADICTION, a contract
> rejection, never a measured COST_FAILURE. Raw news or historical sentiment
> required now remains BLOCKED_DATA_REQUIREMENT, pending data and a reviewed
> catalog extension. Do not silently execute an unsupported event-conditioned rule.
> Check mechanism, support and falsifier against these structured scopes. A claim
> to use a source now while deferring its requirement is a narrative contradiction.
> Preserve it as a material REQUIREMENT_CONTRADICTION objection for operator review.

## How to answer

For each case, give status, route, responsible component, severity, supporting field paths and a short reason. Also state what a correct explanation must mention. Flag invalid or ambiguous records; do not force a binary verdict.

- **SUPPORTED**: a demonstrated requirement-scope defect.
- **NOT_SUPPORTED**: this defect is absent. This does not establish predictive value or admission eligibility.
- **UNRESOLVED**: evidence cannot settle the finding; escalation is required.
- **ASSESSABLE** with reason NONE, or **ESCALATE** with AMBIGUOUS_SCOPE, OUT_OF_CLASS, INVALID_CONTEXT, INTEGRITY_CONFLICT, UNCERTAIN_LINEAGE_OR_EXPOSURE, HIGH_IMPACT or MATERIAL_DISAGREEMENT.
- Components: research, critic, contract_service, test_harness, reference_reviewer, unknown. Context/checker defects are not automatically Research defects.
- Severity: blocking, material or advisory. For an absent defect, material is the neutral rubric value. This convention does not assert an error.

After the individual judgements, review the proposed family ancestry separately. Merge related families where warranted. Fewer than eight supported clusters stops preparation; we will not rename them to obtain independence.
